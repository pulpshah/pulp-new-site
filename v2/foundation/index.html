The Pulp Foundation

A workforce for after AI.

The Pulp Foundation is a nonprofit, 501(c)(3) pending. We prepare an interdisciplinary American workforce for work after AI, with companies across industries.

The apprenticeship

What the Foundation does

Interdisciplinary collaboration starts with self-discipline.

Our programs promote autonomy and critical thinking. Machine learning helps each learner see the fallacies and biases at work in every school of thought, their own included.

The apprenticeship

Students learn by doing real work, side by side with mentors.

Across disciplines

Math, computer science, psychology, political science, business and the arts, in one cohort.

With companies

Partners across industries bring the problems and hire the people.

Open to everyone

Students from any background or campus, however they found the work.

The Pulp AI Apprenticeship

Work on problems no single field owns.

Apprentices take on questions that cut across disciplines, with mentors from other fields and other generations. Their work goes up on Pulp101, and the strongest becomes student research.

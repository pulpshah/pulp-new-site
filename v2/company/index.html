Company

We study how people decide.

Pulp is a research company in Brooklyn, New York. Language, persuasion and attention first; the tools come from what we learn.

Communication holds us together.

People who understand each other, patiently, make the future feel more open.

So we study attention and argument first, then build tools that take load off people's minds instead of adding to it. Years of research; incorporated as Pulp Corporation in 2026.

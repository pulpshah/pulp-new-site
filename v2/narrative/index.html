Questions people ask first

And the one thing we always say: narrative is not a law firm. When you need one, it tells you, and tells you what to ask.

Is narrative a law firm?+

No. narrative prepares and files forms, keeps your record and explains the rules in plain words. When a question needs a lawyer or an accountant, it says so and writes down what to ask them.

Who files: me, or you?+

Either, every time. Submit it yourself at the state's price, or let narrative file it for you at cost. The forms are the same; only the last click changes.

My company already exists. Can I start now?+

Yes. Start at whatever chapter you are in. narrative reads your filings and builds the record from there, then puts the next dates on the calendar.

What is the difference between a process and a plan?+

A process is the same sturdy steps for everyone, like filing an annual report. A plan is the choices only you can make, like who to hire and when. narrative runs the first and remembers the second.

What does it cost?+

The state's and the IRS's fees at cost, with nothing added, plus a subscription for the writing, checking and remembering: [price to be set].

What happens to my record if I leave?+

It is yours. Download every page, filing, receipt and decision, any time, in formats any accountant can open.

A Pulp product · Thinkwell

A workstation that sharpens how a person thinks.

Built for the people whose work runs on persuasion: public affairs teams, agencies, political committees and analysts in the markets. It does not hand them an answer. It sharpens theirs. Launching for business in 2027.

Visit Thinkwell

  1. CraftWrites the work: articles, emails, releases, audiences.
  2. ListenReads news, social media and government releases for claims and stance.
  3. SimulateShows what a persona would say, and what they are really thinking.
  4. ResearchTracks legislation, courts, agencies and markets from the public record.
  5. ScoreMeasures the work against the objective you set, with reasons.
  6. DeliverSchedules and sends posts, articles, releases and email.

A Pulp special project · typodojo

Draw a letter. Make it a font.

A place to learn to draw letters by hand, turn them into a real font, and share it or sell it, with a daily practice and ranks that mark how far you have come. Its first rule: the hand stays the author.

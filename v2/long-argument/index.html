The Long Argument

  • Persuasion runs the world, and it has never been measured. This is the story of the people who built the instrument's parts without knowing it.
  • One hundred seats in seven parts, from Ptahhotep in Egypt around 2400 BCE to the authors of the attention paper in 2017.
  • Every seat has one real picture and three answers: what they contributed, how it works, and why it matters now.
  • Seat 101 is Pulp, founded in Brooklyn in 2019 to put the pieces together. After it come Pulp's apprentices and the Foundation.

How the pictures were chosen

A real likeness, or the real thing.

Each seat gets one picture. A likeness made in their lifetime when one survives; otherwise a real object, page or place from their life or work. Never a portrait imagined centuries later, no stock, no AI. Pictures come under free licenses only, public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA, and every credit the license asks for is printed under its picture.

Every picture is printed once as a halftone in paper ink on Pulp's violet: a 9 pixel screen for a face, 6 for a page, object or place, so writing stays legible. The source files are untouched; each print links back to its source. A share-alike photo's print is shared under the same license.

101 of 101 seats have their picture. Seat 101 is Shah's own photograph, uncropped, at the finer screen. The apprentices' pictures come later.

The apprenticeship

A team built by teaching

Since 2023, more than 100 apprentices from campuses including Princeton, MIT, Duke, Yale, Dartmouth and NYU have put more than 15,000 hours into Pulp. Most were between 17 and 24. They studied math, computer science, psychology, political science, business and the arts. Two of them now work on Pulp's core research team. The newest, Milana Kumar, tells her story in her own words.

Technology doesn't make you a better thinker. It never has. Habits do.

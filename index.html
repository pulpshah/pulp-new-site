Pulp Corporation · EST. 2026 · Brooklyn, New York

We help humans understand humans.

Pulp is a research company in Brooklyn. We measure how people talk, argue, and make up their minds, build tools for people whose work runs on conversation, and publish what we find.

Why it matters

Technology doesn’t make you a better thinker.

It never has. Habits do. Every new tool changes what people practice, and AI is no different.

AI doesn’t care about people. People do, and they’re who we build for: people who want to argue better, listen longer, and change their minds for the right reasons.

Walking away solves nothing.

We’d rather do the work than sit it out: make it clearer what people actually said, what they meant by it, and what the machine did with it.

Three ways we look at people.

Hover or tap a drawing to see what it measures.

Large groups over time: how views move across a city, a country, a decade.

One person at one moment: who they are, what they care about, how they’re feeling, and where they are.

Pulp, on your server.

The Pulp API puts our engine on your server: four modules, one parameterized language for people and machines. MCP gives AI assistants the same access, with scopes you set.

The server SDK, named for the ground, from the light above it to the core.

  • Radiance

    References, rhetorical mass, and persuasive force.

  • Windfall

    Rewards, moderation, and social media connectivity.

  • TrueAura

    Stateful persona modeling and persuasive resonance, scored against an objective.

  • Groundwater

    Machine health: websoil (SEO, interaction data, the top level), domaincrust, codemantle and servercore.

Anchors and cues.

An anchor says where. A cue says what to do there: talk, answer, vote, take a side, react, or back a claim with a source.

Coming

Anchors: where a cue shows up

  • Text span

    A run of words, from one to a paragraph.

  • Image point or area

    A point on a picture, or an area drawn around part of it.

  • Video timestamp

    A moment in a video or an audio file, or a stretch between two moments.

  • Chart label or plot

    A label, a single data point, a series, or a range on an axis.

  • Diagram object

    Any object in an SVG: a node, an edge, a group, or a label.

Cues: what shows up

  • Invite to open discussion

    An open loop: anyone can start talking about the anchor.

  • Prompt to a specific discussion

    A question the author chose, tied to the anchor.

  • Poll

    Three forms: a checkbox group, select one, or fill in the blank.

  • Stance

    A single claim with exactly two sides.

  • React

    A set of labels the author defines, like Clear, Unclear, Convincing, or Needs a source.

  • Fact

    A claim that asks for a source.
